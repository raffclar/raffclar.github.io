Something I've been working on for a while, [Fluidity](https://cms.zanidrak.com/fluidity/) is an interactive water simulator. Uses a 2D heightfield to represent water.

![Fluidity](/assets/fluid/fluid_01.png)

You can pour water onto the terrain, sculpt the ground with circle, mound and plateau brushes, and watch the water flow, pool and cascade in real time.

* Under the hood it is a shallow-water solver using HLL fluxes, with well-balancing so a still lake stays still over uneven ground
* Adaptive CFL-based timestep. Gravity, bottom friction, viscosity, surface tension and adhesion are all settings you can tune.
* Live stats show volume, active cells and FPS, and there is a kinetic energy overlay for debugging the flow.
